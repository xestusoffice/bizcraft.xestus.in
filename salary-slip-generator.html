Why BizCraft Cannot Be Hacked

Traditional accounting portals store client lists, bank details, and invoice amounts in centralized SQL cloud databases prone to ransomware and unauthorized leaks. Xestus BizCraft has zero backend servers and zero remote databases. Your financial ledger executes in your device's isolated V8 memory space.

Intra-State vs Inter-State GST

Under Indian GST rules, transactions where supplier and recipient are in the same state trigger CGST (50%) + SGST (50%). Interstate supplies trigger IGST (100%). BizCraft handles both regimes effortlessly, displaying proper HSN/SAC codes, reverse charge mechanism tags, and auto-computed tax breakdowns.

HRA Tax Exemption Section 10(13A)

Salaried taxpayers claiming House Rent Allowance under Section 10(13A) must produce authentic receipts. If annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000, disclosing the Landlord's PAN is mandatory under IT Act Circular 08/2013. BizCraft automatically validates PAN formats and generates legal receipts with simulated ₹1 revenue stamps.
